MagisGate is a lightweight root app manager for Magisk. It gives you a clean, fast way to control which apps are allowed root access — directly from your phone, without digging through the Magisk database.

Features:
• Grant or revoke Magisk superuser access for any installed app
• Browse all installed apps with name, package, APK size, install/update time, and system/user badges
• Search, filter, and sort apps (system/user, enabled/disabled)
• Built-in operation log for every root action
• Material 3 design, built with Jetpack Compose

Requirements:
• Rooted device with Magisk installed
• Root (superuser) permission granted to MagisGate

MagisGate is free and open source software, licensed under the GNU GPL v3.
